slide.ng themes

The five official themes, each as a live deck. Press the arrow keys to move through a deck.

  • adevThe look of angular.dev: Inter type, Angular gradients, alerts, cards and code blocks, light and dark.
  • seriphSerif fonts and a blue-grey accent, ported from the Slidev theme.
  • shibainuA dark theme in brown and tan, ported from the Slidev theme.
  • bricksFlat coloured shapes with a serif title font, ported from the Slidev theme.
  • apple-basicA plain white theme with photo layouts, ported from the Slidev theme.
Source on GitHub · Theme gallery in the docs · slide.ng is a community project, not affiliated with Slidev or the Angular team.
